FastFix Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy explains how FastFix (Fastrackfix, Corp., a Delaware corporation, "FastFix," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the FastFix dashboard at https://fastfix-dashboard.vercel.app, the FastFix MCP server, plugin, and related services (the "Service"). Questions: sebastian@fastfix.ai.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number (used for SMS login codes), and login identifiers.
  • Connected-account data you authorize: calendar events, tasks derived from your email, and subscription/billing records from accounts you connect (for example, Google). We access only what is needed for the features you use.
  • Content you or your AI agents submit: tasks, events, subscriptions, routine run logs, notes, and attachments.
  • Payment information: handled by Stripe. We receive limited details (such as plan, status, and last four card digits), not your full card number.
  • Usage and device data: log data, IP address, browser/device type, and how the Service is used.

2. How we use information

To provide, operate, secure, and support the Service; authenticate you; process payments; send service and billing notices; troubleshoot; prevent abuse; comply with law; and improve the Service using de-identified or aggregated usage data that cannot reasonably identify you.

3. Google user data (Limited Use)

FastFix's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, we: - use Google user data only to provide and improve user-facing features of the Service you requested; - do not sell Google user data, or use or transfer it for advertising, credit-worthiness, or lending purposes; - do not use Google user data to train generalized AI/ML models; - do not allow humans to read Google user data except with your consent, for security or abuse investigations, to comply with law, or when aggregated and anonymized for internal operations; - transfer it only as needed to provide the Service, comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you.

You can revoke FastFix's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

4. How we share information

We do not sell or share personal information for money or for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only: - with service providers (subprocessors) that process it on our behalf under contract: Vercel (hosting), Supabase (database and authentication), Stripe (payments), Twilio (SMS login codes), and AI model providers (to process requests you or your agents make; they may not use your data to train their models where we can contractually require that); - with third-party services you choose to connect, at your direction; - to comply with law, enforce our Terms, or protect rights, safety, and security; - in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

We may share de-identified or aggregated information that does not identify you.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep personal information while your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. After you close your account or request deletion, we delete or de-identify your data within 30 days, except where we must keep it for legal, tax, billing, security, or dispute purposes. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis. To request deletion, email sebastian@fastfix.ai.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, including encryption in transit, access controls, and least-privilege access. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Your rights

California (CCPA/CPRA): you may request to know/access, correct, and delete your personal information, and to opt out of sale or sharing (we do not sell or share). We do not use sensitive personal information beyond what is permitted to provide the Service, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. You may use an authorized agent. EEA/UK (GDPR): you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and object to processing, and may complain to your local supervisory authority. Our legal bases are performance of a contract, legitimate interests (security, improvement), consent (e.g., connected accounts, which you can withdraw), and legal obligations. Your data is processed in the United States; where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. To exercise any right, email sebastian@fastfix.ai. We will verify your request and respond within the time required by law.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version with a new effective date and, for material changes, notify you by email or in the Service.
