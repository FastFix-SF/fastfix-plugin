# FastFix Privacy Policy
_Last updated: [DATE]_

FastFix ("we") provides the FastFix dashboard and its MCP server/plugin.

**What we collect:** account info (name, email), content you or your agents store in the dashboard (calendar events, tasks, subscriptions, routine run logs, attached proofs), connector configuration, billing info (processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers), and basic usage/log data.

**How we use it:** to run the service, authenticate you, show your data to you and the agents you authorize, provide support and paid customization, bill you, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your personal data and do not use your content to train AI models.

**Sharing:** only with service providers that host or operate FastFix (e.g. hosting, database, payments, email), when you connect a third-party service, or when required by law.

**Retention & deletion:** we keep data while your account is active. Email [privacy@fastfix.ai] to export or delete your data; we delete it within 30 days of account closure except where law requires otherwise.

**Security:** OAuth-based access, TLS in transit, access limited to authorized staff.

**Your rights:** depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, CCPA) you may access, correct, delete, or port your data. Contact [privacy@fastfix.ai].

**Changes:** we will post updates here and change the date above.
